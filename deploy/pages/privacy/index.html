6. Your rights

Under Law 09-08 you can ask to access your data, correct it, object to its processing for legitimate reasons, and have it deleted. You can delete your account yourself in the app; see Delete your account. For anything else, write to [privacy email]; we answer within [30 days]. You can also complain to the Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel (CNDP), www.cndp.ma.

7. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your phone; the courier app keeps its session in the phone's secure storage. Staff access is limited to what their role needs. Delivery PINs are shown only to the customer and Yallo staff, never to couriers or stores.

8. Children

Yallo is not meant for children under [16/18]. Couriers must be adults.
