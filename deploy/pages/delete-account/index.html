Delete your Yallo account

For the Yallo customer app (ma.yallo.app) and the Yallo Courier app (ma.yallo.courier).

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in with your phone number.
  2. Go to Profile, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every device.

Without the app

Send a message from the phone number of the account, through in-app Help & support, or by email to [support email to be set], saying “Delete my Yallo account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by SMS or email.

When deletion has to wait

  • An order in progress. Customers can delete their account once the order is delivered or cancelled.
  • Couriers on a delivery or with a job offer waiting. Finish or answer it first.
  • Couriers holding cash collected from customers. Hand it in to Yallo first; the app shows the amount.

What is deleted

  • Your account and all your sign-in sessions; notification tokens.
  • Your name (replaced by “Deleted user”), phone number, delivery addresses, GPS positions, delivery and kitchen notes, and the comments you left with ratings.
  • Your name on chat messages and support tickets (shown as “Deleted user”).
  • Couriers: name, phone number and vehicle plate on the courier record and the application.

What is kept, and why

  • Order records (date, store, items, amounts, status), without your name or contact details, because the law requires them for accounting. They are kept for the accounting retention period (see the privacy policy), then deleted.
  • Courier earnings and payout records, anonymised, for the same reason.

Your phone number is free again afterwards: signing in with it later starts a new account. Couriers have to apply again.
