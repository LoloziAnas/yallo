## Full description (≤ 4000)

Yallo brings Marrakech to your door: tajines from Guéliz, fresh produce from the souk, pastries for breakfast,
the pharmacy round the corner.

**Order in a few taps**
• Browse restaurants, groceries, pharmacies, bakeries, shops and drinks near you
• Choose portions and extras, add a note for the rider, apply a promo code
• Pay cash on delivery

**Know where your order is**
• Follow your order live on the map, from the kitchen to your door
• See your rider, call them or chat with them in the app
• Give your rider a 4-digit delivery code at the door, so your order reaches you and no one else

**Made for Marrakech**
• Find your address on the map, by searching a street, or with your location
• Add a landmark ("opposite the mosque", "blue door") so riders find you
• In English, French and Arabic

**After delivery**
• Rate your order
• Reorder your favourites in one tap
• Get help from the Yallo team when something goes wrong

Yallo currently delivers in Marrakech.
