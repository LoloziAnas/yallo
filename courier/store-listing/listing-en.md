## Full description (≤ 4000)

Yallo Courier is the app for Yallo's delivery partners in Marrakech. Go online when it suits you, take the
deliveries you want, and see what you earn on every trip.

**Get delivery requests near you**
When you go online, Yallo sends you orders from restaurants, groceries and pharmacies close to where you are.
Each request shows what you will earn, the distance, the pickup and the drop-off before you accept. You have a
few seconds to accept or decline; declining is fine.

**Clear pay, before you accept**
Every job shows its pay up front: a base amount plus a rate per kilometre for the whole trip. Customer tips go
to you in full. Your earnings, tips and cash collected are updated after every delivery, with a weekly payout
summary.

**From pickup to door**
- A live map of your way to the store and to the customer.
- One tap to open turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps or Waze.
- The order number to show at the counter, and a checklist of the items in the bag.
- Call the store or the customer, or chat with the customer in the app.
- The customer's delivery PIN confirms each hand-over, and cash orders show exactly how much to collect.

**Help when you need it**
Report a problem with an order, reach Yallo support from the app, and follow the conversation until it's
solved.

**Your language**
Français, العربية and English.

**About location**
Yallo uses your location while you're online to send you nearby deliveries. During a delivery it keeps sharing
your location, including with the screen off, so the customer and the Yallo team can follow the order. A
notification shows whenever location is being shared, and it stops when you go offline.

To use Yallo Courier you need a Yallo courier account. New couriers can apply from the app; the Yallo team
checks your documents before your first delivery.
